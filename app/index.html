Roadmap to the Platinum

Six stages, in order. Story first (around 60 hours), then 100%, collectibles, activities, money and property, and a final cleanup of missables and one-offs. Press estimate for the platinum: roughly 200 hours. It is long, not hard.

    Missable Trophies — 29 to protect

    The game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus (reported at launch), so every one-shot story moment is effectively missable. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Each flag below is labelled “treat as missable until confirmed” where its window is not confirmed by any source. The one confirmed case: a Chapter 1 meditation spot can be lost after that chapter.

      Sources & honesty notes

      • Official names, descriptions and grades: the PlayStation list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked with TrueAchievements.
      • Guides cite their sources beside each trophy: PlanetGTA6, Output Lag, Vice City Hub, Leonida Hub, PowerPyx, and the PSNProfiles and PowerPyx GTA V guides for series mechanics that carry over.
      • Every trophy is single-player. There are no online trophies at launch.
      • Cheats disable trophies (reported by PowerPyx; Rockstar Support confirmed the same rule for GTA V).
      • Rockstar has not spelled out what 100% completion covers. This guide says so instead of inventing a checklist.
      • Progress is saved in your browser only (localStorage). Export and import move it as a JSON file. This guide works fully offline.
